Biography & Background

About NfgOdin

Tinkerer, software developer, and lifelong gamer.

The Wilderness Computer Salvage (Age 13)

My lifelong fascination with computers didn't start in a computer science academy or from an off-the-shelf retail purchase. When I was 13 years old, I stumbled across an abandoned, discarded computer tower left out in the desert wilderness.

Instead of passing it by, I dragged it home. Without formal training, I opened the case, studied the ribbon cables, cleaned the components, and troubleshot the motherboard and drives until I successfully brought the system to life running Windows 98. Rescuing that machine gave me a deep appreciation for understanding what happens underneath the hood of computing hardware.

Gaming Roots: Final Fantasy VII & MMORPGs

I have always had a deep love for RPGs. The very first RPG I ever completed was Final Fantasy VII on the original PlayStation back in 1998 or 1999. It left an indelible mark on my imagination, storytelling sensibilities, and love for technical gaming mechanics.

In 2007, I took my hardware knowledge to the next level by buying individual parts on eBay and assembling my own custom gaming PC from scratch. That machine unlocked a brand new world for me: MMORPGs, online gaming communities, and eventually the desire to mod, hook, and modernize classic titles like Final Fantasy VII and X.

AI as a Tool: The Power of Leverage

I love artificial intelligence. My deep dive into AI really kicked into high gear when local Stable Diffusion models became accessible and easy to run on consumer hardware—specifically on the RTX 3060 GPU I had at the time. Setting up local checkpoints, prompting, optimizing inference, and seeing generative models run right on my own machine blew the doors wide open.

My philosophy on AI is simple: Why eat cold cereal with your bare hands when you have a tool called a spoon that helps you eat it? The exact same principle applies to technology. Why struggle through every repetitive, manual task by hand when you can leverage intelligent tools to accelerate your workflow, solve problems faster, and focus your energy on what truly matters?

Technical Skills & Credentials

Over the years I have balanced self-taught low-level engineering with focused coursework:

  • Programming Courses: College-level coursework in Java Programming and C# Programming.
  • Networking: Cisco Networking Certificate from taking the college course in high school.
  • Cybersecurity: Google Cybersecurity online coursework and certifications.
  • Languages & Toolchains: C++20, Python 3, Java, Win32 API, glTF 2.0 3D Pipelines, Linux / Raspberry Pi 5.
